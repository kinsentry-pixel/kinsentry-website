Features

Everything to keep them safe

Built specifically to protect elderly users from the growing threat of remote access tech support scams.

Remote Access Site Blocking

Automatically blocks 70+ remote desktop software sites including AnyDesk, TeamViewer, UltraViewer, and more. Updated regularly.

Remote & Malicious Software Download Blocker

Intercepts downloads of remote-access tools and known malicious software before they reach the device — stopping scammers from installing control software in the first place.

Voice Warning Alerts

A clear, spoken warning plays instantly when a scam site is detected. Impossible to miss, even for users unfamiliar with pop-ups.

ScamAd Detector

Automatically detects fake ads on Google, Bing, Yahoo and DuckDuckGo that impersonate trusted brands like Norton, McAfee and Microsoft — and warns the user before they click.

Scam Page & Popup Blocking

Detects and blocks fake tech support pages, deceptive pop-ups, and fraudulent "virus detected" alerts before they can trick your loved ones into calling scam numbers.

Phishing Email Detection

Scans incoming emails for phishing links, spoofed senders, and fraudulent "account suspended" or "payment failed" scams — flagging dangerous messages before they're opened.

Family Alert System

Instantly notifies family members via email when a scam attempt is blocked. Stay informed from anywhere.

Full-Screen Overlay

Unmissable visual alert covers the entire screen with clear instructions. CSS-isolated so no website can interfere with it.

Tamper-Resistant

Page refresh bypass protection ensures the warning re-appears even if the scammer instructs the user to reload the page.

Activity Dashboard

Track blocked attempts, view history, and monitor protection status across all family devices from one central dashboard.

Real-Time Scam Number Alerts

Get instant push notifications and email alerts when new scam phone numbers are identified. Stay ahead of emerging threats with our continuously updated scam database.
