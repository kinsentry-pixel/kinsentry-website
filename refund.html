Refund Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Our Commitment

We want you to be completely satisfied with KinSentry. If you're not happy with your subscription, we offer a straightforward refund process.

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are unsatisfied with a paid subscription, you may request a full refund of an eligible charge within 30 days of the date that charge was made. The 30-day period starts with your first successful paid charge, not when you begin the no-card Guardian trial. An immediate prorated charge for a Guardian-to-Family upgrade is also eligible for a refund request within 30 days of that upgrade charge. No reason is required. This policy does not limit any refund rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.

After 30 Days

After 30 days from a charge, that charge is not refundable under this policy. You may request cancellation at any time to prevent the next renewal. To cancel, contact KinSentry at billing@kinsentry.com with your account email and ask us to cancel before your renewal date. If cancellation is scheduled, paid features remain available until the end of the current paid billing period.

Guardian-to-Family Upgrades

When you choose to upgrade from Guardian to Family, we show you the estimated prorated charge and the saved payment method before asking you to confirm. Stripe charges the prorated difference immediately after confirmation. Your Family subscription then renews at the Family price on your existing renewal date. If you do not confirm, the upgrade is not made and no upgrade charge is initiated.

Annual Subscriptions

The same 30-day refund window applies to monthly and annual paid charges. After that window, cancelling an annual subscription prevents its next renewal, but does not provide a prorated refund for unused time in the current annual period.

How to Request a Refund

Email billing@kinsentry.com with the email address on your KinSentry account and the order or invoice number, if available. You do not need to provide a reason. We will review your request within 5-7 business days. If approved, we will submit the refund through Stripe; the time for the credit to appear depends on your bank or payment provider.

Free Trial

KinSentry offers a 30-day Guardian trial only. It is managed by KinSentry, requires no payment card, creates no Stripe subscription, and incurs no charge. Family has no trial. If you do not choose a paid plan, your account returns to the Free tier without a charge. If you choose a paid plan, the 30-day refund period begins on the date of your first successful payment; the Guardian trial period does not count toward it.

Contact

For billing questions, email billing@kinsentry.com.
