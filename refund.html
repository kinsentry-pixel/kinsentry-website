Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Our Commitment

We want you to be completely satisfied with KinSentry. If you're not happy with your subscription, we offer a straightforward refund process.

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

You may request a full refund of an eligible successful paid charge within 30 days of the date it was made. Eligible charges include monthly or annual Guardian and Family subscription payments, including renewal payments, and any positive prorated charge billed immediately for a Guardian-to-Family upgrade. The 30-day period for each charge starts on the date that charge was made. Your no-card Guardian trial is not a paid charge and does not count toward this period. No reason is required. This policy does not limit any refund rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.

After 30 Days

After 30 days from a charge, that charge is not refundable under this policy. A refund request and subscription cancellation are separate: a refund does not by itself cancel your subscription or stop its next renewal. To prevent the next renewal, open Subscription > Manage subscription & billing in your KinSentry account. If cancellation is scheduled, paid features remain available until the end of the current paid billing period. You can also contact billing@kinsentry.com for help.

Guardian-to-Family Upgrades

When you choose to upgrade from Guardian to Family, we show you Stripe’s estimated prorated amount due and saved payment method before asking you to confirm. The amount may be zero or a credit; only a positive amount is billed immediately after confirmation. Your Family subscription then renews at the Family price on your existing renewal date. If a positive upgrade charge is made, you may request a refund of that charge within 30 days. A refund does not by itself reverse the upgrade or cancel the subscription; contact billing@kinsentry.com if you want help changing your plan, and cancel separately to stop renewal. If you do not confirm, the upgrade is not made and no upgrade charge is initiated.

Annual Subscriptions

The same 30-day refund window applies to monthly and annual paid charges. After that window, cancelling an annual subscription prevents its next renewal, but does not provide a prorated refund for unused time in the current annual period.

How to Request a Refund

Email billing@kinsentry.com with the email address on your KinSentry account and the order or invoice number, if available. You do not need to provide a reason. We will review your request within 5-7 business days. If approved, we will submit the refund through Stripe; the time for the credit to appear depends on your bank or payment provider.

Free Trial

KinSentry offers a 30-day Guardian trial only. It is managed by KinSentry, requires no payment card, creates no Stripe subscription, and incurs no charge. Family has no trial. If you do not choose a paid plan, your account returns to the Free tier without a charge. If you choose a paid plan, the 30-day refund period begins on the date of your first successful payment; the Guardian trial period does not count toward it.

Contact

For billing questions, email billing@kinsentry.com.
