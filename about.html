About Us

Protecting the people who raised us

KinSentry was born from a simple belief: no one should lose their life savings to a scam that could have been prevented.

Our Mission

Every day, thousands of elderly people are targeted by tech support scammers who trick them into granting remote access to their computers. The scammers empty bank accounts, steal personal information, and leave families devastated.

KinSentry exists to make this impossible. Our Chrome extension blocks remote access scam sites in real-time, plays loud voice warnings, and instantly alerts family members — creating a safety net that works even when you can't be there.

Our Story

KinSentry started when our founder watched a family member nearly fall victim to a tech support scam. A fake "Microsoft" pop-up, a phone call to a scam number, and instructions to download AnyDesk — it was textbook, and it almost worked.

We realized that the technology to prevent this already existed — it just hadn't been built with the right people in mind. So we built KinSentry: simple enough for anyone to use, powerful enough to stop scammers in their tracks.

Why KinSentry?

"Kin" means family. "Sentry" means guardian. Together, KinSentry is your family's digital guardian — always watching, always protecting.

  • Built specifically for protecting elderly users
  • Two layers of defense: scam page blocking + remote access blocking
  • Real-time family alerts via SMS, email, and WhatsApp
  • Voice warnings that are impossible to miss
  • Tamper-resistant — scammers can't disable it

Built with Care

KinSentry is built in India and serves families in the US, UK, and Canada. We're a small team passionate about elder safety, and we believe that protecting vulnerable people online shouldn't be expensive or complicated.
